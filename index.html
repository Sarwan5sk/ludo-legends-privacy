Privacy Policy — Ludo Legends

App: Ludo Legends
Developer: Cotech Agency, India
Effective date: 3 November 2026
Contact: info@cotechagency.com

This policy explains what information the Ludo Legends app does and does not collect, and how it is used. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. The short version

Ludo Legends has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no in-app purchases. Most of the game (Play vs Computer, pass-and-play on one phone, and local Wi-Fi games) works without any of your information leaving your device or your local network. Only the Online mode sends gameplay data to a server, because that is how the players in a room stay in sync.

2. Information stored on your device only

The app stores the following on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and it is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app:

  • Your sound and music settings
  • A saved game in progress (board positions, scores) so you can resume it
  • Your last game-setup choices (player count, colours, difficulty)

3. Information used in multiplayer games

Local Wi-Fi games. Games are played directly between phones connected to the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot. The app uses the Android "nearby Wi-Fi devices" and network permissions only to find and connect to the host phone. Your chosen display name, colour and moves are exchanged directly between the phones in the room. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.

Online games. Online rooms are synced through Google Firebase Realtime Database, a cloud service provided by Google. When you create or join an online room, the following is transmitted to and stored in that database so the game can work:

  • The display name you choose for the lobby (type any name you like — please do not use your real full name)
  • Your chosen colour and the room code
  • Gameplay data: dice results, moves, and the current board state of the match

Other players in your room can see your display name, colour, and moves — that is part of playing the game together. Online rooms are temporary: room data is kept only for operating the service and may be deleted at any time without notice. Google may also process technical information such as IP addresses and anonymous device/installation identifiers as part of providing Firebase; that processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or accounts are collected by us
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access
  • No precise location (the nearby-devices permission is used only to discover the game host on your local network; no location is read, stored or sent)
  • No advertising identifiers, no behavioural tracking, no selling of data to anyone

5. How information is used

Solely to run the game: syncing multiplayer matches, resuming your saved game, and remembering your settings. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the game, and we never sell personal information.

6. Sharing

We share data only with the service provider that makes Online mode possible (Google Firebase), as described above, and when required by law. Other players in a multiplayer room see your in-game display name, colour and moves.

7. Children

The app does not require any personal information to play and contains no chat or social features. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because an online display name can be any text a player types, we recommend that players — especially children — use a made-up name. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a display name and want it removed, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data sent to Firebase travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS). Data stored on your device stays in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox.

9. Your choices

  • Play the offline and local modes if you prefer that no data leaves your device or local network.
  • Erase all locally stored data at any time: in the app use Settings → Clear Saved Game, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • To request deletion of online room data associated with a display name you used, contact us at the email above.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Play Store listing.
